# R² · Rocky Robotics: 1-minute demo script

**Setup:** laptop shows `/view` (aerial quarry on the left, truck + excavator cams on the right). The phone radio is open and handed to a judge. Trucks start parked, excavators holding.

| Time | Say | Do / show |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | "Quarries move 39 billion tonnes of rock a year, almost all with manned trucks. The autonomy that exists was built for giant 24/7 mines and costs about $2.3M a truck. Mid-size quarries are left out." | Aerial view: the real drone-scanned quarry |
| 0:10–0:20 | "R² retrofits the trucks they already own with a sensor pod, and you run the whole site by **talking** to it." | Hold up the sensor pod and hand the phone to a judge |
| 0:20–0:35 | (Judge, on the radio) **"Excavator one, start digging."** then **"Truck one, go unload at excavator one."** | Excavator swings from the rock face to the truck bed; the radio replies in each machine's voice; Truck One pulls out of the ready line on the haul road |
| 0:35–0:45 | "Their words become goals; a vision policy drives on camera and lidar. This site is a real drone scan, turned into a sim in ten seconds: roads, berms and grades to industry standard, now running in NVIDIA Isaac Sim." | Point at the cab cams and the road ribbons |
| 0:45–0:55 | "The economics: a quarry driver is up to a third of truck running cost. Autonomy operators report 13–20% lower haul cost. A 10-truck quarry on two shifts pays back our kit in about a year, and in four months running 24/7." | — |
| 0:55–1:00 | "Safety first:" (judge or you) **"Everyone stop."** "Every machine halts. R², talk to your quarry." | All trucks stop and excavators hold |

**Backup lines if asked**
- Market: 3,832 autonomous haul trucks worldwide (+84% in a year), almost none under 100 t. US aggregates is about $40B across ~10,000 quarries and pits.
- Model: $90k kit per truck, $75k site onboarding, $4k per truck per month. Seed ask $7M for 3 paid pilots, an ISO 17757 safety case and the team.
- Safety: powered haulage is 25–43% of US mining deaths (MSHA).
- Competition: Pronto (now Atoms), Sensmore and Caterpillar. Our wedge is any-site onboarding from a drone scan plus natural-language ops.
- Honest status: sim, radio, voice and the policy loop are built; the hardware pod is a concept; pricing is our assumption.
