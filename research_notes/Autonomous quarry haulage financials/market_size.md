# Market Size: Autonomous Haulage / Autonomous Mining Equipment and the Aggregates & Quarry Market

Research date: 2026-10-17. The year of every figure is shown in brackets. Analyst-report figures come from vendor summary pages, not the full paid reports. They are directional, and their scopes differ a lot.

## 1. Global AHS / autonomous mining equipment market size and CAGR

### Takeaway
Published estimates of the AHS market for 2025 run from about $0.5B to $4.3B. Projected CAGRs run from about 7% to 22%. If the definition widens to "autonomous mining equipment", the 2024 figures are $3.7B–4.5B, plus one outlier at $81B. The spread comes almost entirely from scope: some count the full truck value, others only the autonomy kit and software. A startup should not quote the top-line numbers as its addressable market.

### Cited Findings
- **MarketsandMarkets (AHS):** $4.30B [2025], rising to $17.30B by 2032. CAGR 22.0% [2026–2032]. By region in 2025: APAC $1.72B, North America $1.20B, Rest of World $1.08B, Europe $0.30B. Its segments include "autonomous haul trucks and vehicles", so the full truck value is counted. Fleet-management and autonomy software is called the fastest-growing component. Retrofit and quarry are mentioned as trends but not sized. Named players include Caterpillar, Komatsu, Hitachi, Wenco, Sandvik, Epiroc, Modular Mining, ASI Mining, Pronto.ai, Volvo, Scania and Hexagon. — [MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/autonomous-mining-haulage-systems-market-266401441.html)
- **Marketintelo (AHS):** $3.8B [2025], rising to $14.7B by 2034. CAGR 14.2%. — [Marketintelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/autonomous-haulage-system-market)
- **Emergen Research (autonomous mining trucks and haulage systems):** $3.5B [2025]. CAGR 8.9% to 2035. — [Emergen Research](https://www.emergenresearch.com/index.php/industry-report/autonomous-mining-trucks-and-haulage-systems-market)
- **Intel Market Research (AHS):** $543M [2025], rising to $903M by 2031. CAGR 9.1%. This is probably the system and kit value only. — [Intel Market Research](https://www.intelmarketresearch.com/autonomous-haulage-systems-market-9613)
- **"Mine unmanned transportation systems" (publisher cited by an aggregator):** $651M [2025], rising to $1,045M by 2032. CAGR 7.1%. — [foriio summary](https://foriio.com/works/2247597) (secondary source, low reliability)
- **Market.us (autonomous mining equipment):** $3.7B [2024], rising to $9.5B by 2034. CAGR 9.9%. Hardware was 47.8% and APAC 42.8% [2024]. — [Market.us](https://scoop.market.us/autonomous-mining-equipment-market-news/)
- **Grand View Research (autonomous mining equipment, via a GII reseller listing):** roughly $4.48B [2024], rising to $11.86B by 2033. CAGR 11.6% [2025–2033]. The search snippet tied these figures to the listing but I did not verify them on Grand View's own page. — [GII / Grand View listing](https://giikorea.co.kr/report/grvi1842199-autonomous-mining-equipment-market-size-share.html)
- **Value Market Research (autonomous mining equipment):** $81.49B [2025], rising to $195.64B by 2034. CAGR 10.22%. This is an outlier that probably includes all equipment carrying any automation. — [GII / VMR listing](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/vmr2096364-global-autonomous-mining-equipment-market-size.html)
- **Context, Frost & Sullivan (all mining equipment, not just autonomous):** $62.23B [2024]. — [Frost & Sullivan](https://store.frost.com/mining-equipment-industry-global-2024-2030.html)

### Inferences
- The estimates fall into two groups: about $0.5–0.65B (autonomy systems only) and about $3.5–4.5B (including the autonomous trucks themselves). For a retrofit kit plus software business, the roughly $0.5–0.9B "systems" estimates are the better proxy for today's spend on autonomy itself. Most of that spend comes from big OEM-AHS mines, not quarries.
- Analysts disagree mostly on scope. Growth rates also vary widely: about 7–9% from the conservative publishers versus 22% from MarketsandMarkets.

### Gaps
- I did not find primary pages for Fortune Business Insights, IDTechEx or Mordor AHS figures, and none of the pages I fetched split retrofit from new-build or quarry from mining.
- All the analyst figures come from paywalled reports. Their methods are not visible.

## 2. Autonomous haul trucks deployed vs the total installed base

### Takeaway
GlobalData counted about 3,832 autonomous haul trucks operating worldwide in July 2025, up 84% from 2,080 in July 2024. China drove most of the jump (about 2,090 units). Caterpillar had 827 trucks in operation at end-2025, and Komatsu passed 1,000 commissioned ultra-class trucks in April 2026. Autonomy is still a small minority of the large-truck fleet, and almost all of it is at large coal, iron ore, copper and gold mines.

### Cited Findings
- **Global count:** 3,832 autonomous haul trucks operating [July 2025], up from 2,080 [July 2024], an 84% increase. GlobalData projects close to 5,000 by end-2030. — [Mining Technology / GlobalData](https://www.mining-technology.com/analyst-comment/china-global-leader-autonomous-surface-mining-trucks/)
- **By country [July 2025]:** China 2,090 (562 in 2024), Australia 1,024 (927 in 2024), Canada 344, Chile 208. — [Mining Technology / GlobalData](https://www.mining-technology.com/analyst-comment/china-global-leader-autonomous-surface-mining-trucks/)
- **OEM concentration:** Caterpillar, Komatsu, Tonly and LGMG supply 88% of the trucks GlobalData tracks. Caterpillar leads and Komatsu is second. The largest operators are CHN Energy (509), Guanghui Energy (420; Baishihu has 420 at one site), BHP (360) and Rio Tinto (305). — [Mining Technology / GlobalData](https://www.mining-technology.com/analyst-comment/china-global-leader-autonomous-surface-mining-trucks/)
- **Earlier baseline:** GlobalData counted just over 1,000 autonomous trucks in 2021 and forecast about 1,800 by 2025. The 3,832 actual in 2025 roughly doubled that forecast. — [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/global-autonomous-mining-truck-population-tops-thousand-mark-to-reach-1800-by-2025-report/)
- **Caterpillar:** 827 autonomous haul trucks in operation [end-2025], up from 690 [end-2024]. Its stated goal is to triple the 2024 autonomous truck population by 2030. — [Caterpillar FY2025 Annual Report (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000018230/000130817926000360/cat015318-ars.pdf); [Caterpillar 2025 segment highlights](https://www.caterpillar.com/content/caterpillarDotCom/en/investors/reports/annual-report/segment-highlights.html)
- **Komatsu:** commissioned its 1,000th autonomous ultra-class FrontRunner truck, a 930E-5AT at Nevada Gold Mines [April 2026]. More than 500 of these are 930Es. FrontRunner trucks have hauled more than 11.5 billion t since commercial launch in 2008. — [Construction Equipment Guide](https://www.constructionequipmentguide.com/komatsu-commissions-1000-ultra-class-autonomous-haul-trucks/71124?markdown=1)
- **Total installed base (dated):** Parker Bay counted nearly 31,000 large trucks (100 t+ payload) and 6,400 large excavators delivered in the decade to 2014, with an installed value of about $80B. It also estimated about 12,000 trucks would reach retirement age in 2015–2020. — [Mining Weekly / Parker Bay (2016)](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/mining-equipment-markets-projected-to-remain-near-2015-lows-in-2016-2016-03-10)

### Inferences
- If the installed base of 100 t+ trucks is roughly 25,000–35,000 (an extrapolation from Parker Bay's 2016 data), the roughly 1,800+ Cat and Komatsu autonomous trucks are about 5–8% of it. Global autonomy penetration is probably around 10% or less of large mining trucks.
- The Chinese surge comes from domestic OEMs and autonomy firms (Tonly, LGMG, EACON and others) at coal mines. Those are hard markets for a Western startup to enter.
- Rigid trucks under 100 t (the 40–100 t quarry class, such as the Cat 770–777 and Komatsu HD465–HD785) and articulated haulers are almost completely non-autonomous today. That is the opening for a retrofit product.

### Gaps
- I found no current public global installed-base count, for either 100 t+ trucks or the smaller 40–100 t rigid class. Parker Bay's current data is behind a subscription.
- I found no recent public counts for Hitachi, Liebherr, Epiroc or Sandvik surface trucks.
- Komatsu's 1,000 is a cumulative commissioned total. Caterpillar's 827 is an operating count at year-end. The two are not like-for-like.

## 3. Aggregates and quarry market: tonnes, revenue, site counts, fleet size

### Takeaway
World aggregates output is about 39 billion t a year [2024], from roughly 500,000 quarries and pits. Earlier estimates said 50 billion t. The US produced about 2.4B short tons worth about $40B in 2025, from about 3,500 crushed stone quarries and 6,500 sand and gravel pits. The EU27 plus EFTA produced about 3B t a year from about 26,000 sites run by about 15,000 mostly small companies, with turnover of up to €20B. No published source gives a standard fleet size for a mid-size quarry. Project examples suggest about 3–12 rigid or articulated haul trucks plus 1–3 loaders or excavators at a 1–5 Mt/yr site.

### Cited Findings
**Global**
- GAIN puts global aggregates production at 44.0 billion t [2019], falling to 39.0 billion t [2024]. It expects 2025 to be about the same at 39.0 billion t. — [Aggregates Business / GAIN](https://aggbusiness.com/feature/global-aggregates-growth-examined-gain-convenor-jim-obrien)
- Regional shares: China 39%, India 15%, Europe 7%, USA 6% (most recent GAIN breakdown). — [Aggregates Business / GAIN](https://www.aggbusiness.com/feature/global-aggregates-demand-despite-strong-headwinds)
- An older GAIN estimate gave 50 billion t a year produced in "around 500,000 quarries and pits worldwide". It also projected 50–55 billion t by 2030, but that projection predates the downward revisions. — [Aggregates Business / GAIN](https://www.aggbusiness.com/feature/growing-global-aggregates-sustainably)

**United States (USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries)**
- **Crushed stone:** 1.5B short tons worth $27B [2025], from about 1,400 companies running 3,500 quarries and more than 180 yards. In 2024 it was 1.5B t worth $26B with the same counts. — [USGS MCS 2026 Crushed Stone](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-stone-crushed.pdf); [USGS MCS 2025 Crushed Stone](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-stone-crushed.pdf)
- **Construction sand and gravel:** 870M short tons worth $12.6B [2025], from about 3,400 companies running 6,500 pits. Uses: 42% concrete aggregate, 20% road base, 12% fill, 9% asphalt. — [USGS MCS 2026 Sand & Gravel](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-sand-gravel.pdf)
- USGS counts come from voluntary surveys. Trade press notes a known undercount. — [Stone World](https://www.stoneworld.com/articles/95709-how-much-natural-stone-does-the-us-actually-quarry)

**Europe (UEPG)**
- EU27 plus EFTA demand is about 3 billion t a year [2021], with turnover of up to €20B, about 15,000 companies (mostly SMEs) and about 187,000 employed. — [UEPG Facts](https://uepg.eu/pages/facts)
- About 26,000 quarries and pits, with 220,000 employed directly and indirectly. — [UEPG home](https://uepg.eu)
- EU27 plus UK plus EFTA produced 2.99B t [2019]. Across 42 European countries the total was 4.20B t [2019]. 87% comes from natural quarries and pits, 8% is recycled. — [UEPG Trends](https://uepg.eu/pages/trends)

**Typical quarry fleet (anecdotal; no standard source)**
- Luck Stone's Bull Run quarry (Virginia) runs 4 autonomous Cat 777s (about 90 t class) [since Nov 2024], which have hauled more than 3.5M tons. — [MarketScale / Equipment World](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/engineering-and-construction/cat-brings-driverless-hauling-on-the-smaller-775-truck-to-two-more-luck-stone-quarries)
- A Canadian project filing planned an initial haul fleet of 11 × 50 t rigid trucks plus a backhoe excavator, dozers, a grader and a water truck [2006]. — [Birch Mountain technical report (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001006224/000120445906000689/bmrtechreport.htm)
- A small Canadian pit runs 2 Volvo EC360B excavators, 5 A25D articulated haulers and 1 EC380D. — [search result, Volvo case](https://gulfconstructiononline.com/Article/379285/Volvo_machines_aid_quarry_revival_in_Northern_Emirates) (attribution uncertain)
- A UK contractor hauls 4 Mt/yr of granite with a mixed Caterpillar and Komatsu truck fleet plus excavators and loaders. — [Agg-Net](https://agg-net.com/news/load-and-haul)

### Inferences
- The average US crushed stone quarry produces about 430,000 short tons a year (1.5B / 3,500). The average sand and gravel pit produces about 134,000 tons a year (870M / 6,500). Most US sites are small. The mid-size segment (about 0.5–5 Mt/yr, roughly 3–12 trucks) is probably a minority of sites, numbering in the hundreds to low thousands.
- Illustrative serviceable market for the US alone, using my own assumptions: if about 1,000–1,500 crushed stone quarries run 3–8 rigid haul trucks each, that is about 3,000–12,000 trucks. At a retrofit kit price of $150–300k per truck (my assumption, unverified), plus software, that gives a one-time hardware pool of roughly $0.5–3.5B, plus recurring software. The same logic applies to the EU's 26,000 sites.
- The aggregates industry's revenue (about $40B in the US and €20B in the EU) is large next to the AHS market. But the spend on haulage autonomy is still nascent.

### Gaps
- I found no published statistic on haul trucks per quarry, or on how quarries are distributed by output size. USGS state-level data or NSSGA might help.
- I found no country-by-country quarry counts outside the US and Europe.

## 4. OEM-locked large mines vs mixed-fleet mid-size quarries; evidence of underservice

### Takeaway
OEM AHS products (Cat MineStar Command, Komatsu FrontRunner) were built for large 24/7 mines with 100 t+ trucks. Caterpillar only reached quarry-class trucks in 2024–2026: 777s at Luck Stone in November 2024, and 775s announced for two more Luck Stone sites in September 2026. Caterpillar itself says it is "learning" how to automate fewer trucks that don't run 24 hours a day. Independent retrofit vendors are already winning quarry business on mixed fleets. The clearest case is Pronto with Heidelberg Materials: more than 100 trucks, mixed Caterpillar and Komatsu. The underserved gap is real but closing, and OEMs are now moving down-market.

### Cited Findings
- Caterpillar says mining autonomy was built around large trucks running 24/7. At Luck Stone it is learning to automate fewer trucks that don't haul around the clock, and to adapt to "smaller operations". The 775 (71 t payload) had never run MineStar autonomy before. Expansion to the Boscobel and Bealeton quarries was announced on 14 September 2026. — [MarketScale / Equipment World](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/engineering-and-construction/cat-brings-driverless-hauling-on-the-smaller-775-truck-to-two-more-luck-stone-quarries)
- Heidelberg Materials agreed to deploy Pronto's retrofit AHS on more than 100 trucks over 3 years at more than a dozen sites in North America, Europe and APAC [February 2025]. The deal makes Pronto its exclusive autonomy provider in North America. The trucks are a mix of new and pre-owned rigid haul trucks. The system is camera-based (no lidar) and deploys in weeks. — [Heidelberg Materials press release](https://www.heidelbergmaterials.com/en/pr-2025-02-24)
- The Pronto pilot at Bridgeport, Texas used a mixed Caterpillar and Komatsu fleet and hauled 2M t in under 8 months. — [Pit & Quarry](https://www.pitandquarry.com/?p=101620); [Rock Products](https://rockproducts.com/2025/02/24/heidelberg-materials-to-deploy-more-than-100-autonomous-haul-trucks/)
- A Pronto and Whittle study argues that bigger is not always better for autonomous trucks: smaller autonomous trucks can beat larger human-driven ones. It was co-sponsored by an AHS vendor. — [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/?p=1124030); [International Mining](https://im-mining.com/?p=97341)
- A 2024 Winter Simulation Conference paper found that autonomous 40 t trucks beat human-driven 100 t trucks on NPV in its scenario (about +31%). — [WSC 2024 paper](https://informs-sim.org/wsc24papers/cea145.pdf)
- Pronto reportedly says its system works with fleets of fewer than 10 trucks in the 30–150 t range. I found no independent confirmation. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/b80dbfbe-1f47-4ec2-b86c-39500a13efcf/)
- Other OEM-agnostic retrofit players:
  - ASI Mining took an order for 18 autonomous haul truck conversion kits [August 2023]. — [Global Mining Review](https://www.globalminingreview.com/technology-digitalisation/14082023/asi-mining-receives-order-for-18-autonomous-haul-truck-conversion-kits/)
  - ASI and Barrick ran a retrofit project in the US. — [E&MJ](https://www.e-mj.com/breaking-news/asi-works-with-barrick-on-first-major-autonomous-haulage-project-in-the-us/)
  - EACON's drive-by-wire retrofit ran driverless on a used Komatsu HD1500 at Norton Gold Fields [January 2026]. — [International Mining](https://im-mining.com/2026/01/12/eacon-confirms-autonomy-milestone-reached-at-norton-gold-fields-mulgarrie/)
  - SafeAI chose retrofit as its approach. — [Metal Tech News](https://metaltechnews.com/story/2021/07/21/mining-tech/safeai-opts-to-retrofit-autonomous-trucks/624.html)

### Inferences
- The concentration among Cat, Komatsu and two Chinese OEMs (88% of autonomous trucks), plus the operator lists dominated by BHP, Rio, Fortescue and Chinese coal majors, shows the installed autonomy base is almost entirely OEM-system fleets at large mines.
- Quarries tend to run mixed-brand, older, smaller trucks and are often single-shift. They don't fit OEM AHS, which is tied to new OEM trucks, high fixed infrastructure costs and 24/7 utilisation. The Pronto–Heidelberg deal confirms both the demand and that retrofit works on mixed fleets.
- Competitive risk: Pronto already has the leading quarry reference customer (Heidelberg). Caterpillar is pushing into the 775/777 quarry class through dealers (Carter Machinery). The startup's window is mid-size independents and mixed-fleet operators. Excavator autonomy is a possible differentiator, since most rivals focus on trucks.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative split of the global fleet between OEM-locked large mines and mixed-fleet quarries.
- I found no published OEM AHS price per truck or per site, and no explicit minimum fleet size. Industry lore says OEM AHS needs large fleets and site infrastructure, but I found no primary source with numbers.

## 5. Retrofit / aftermarket autonomy market estimates

### Takeaway
I found no analyst report that sizes the retrofit or aftermarket autonomy market for construction and mining equipment on its own. MarketsandMarkets mentions retrofit kits as a trend but gives no figure. The best proxies are the "systems-only" AHS estimates (about $0.5–0.9B in 2025) and deal sizes (Heidelberg–Pronto: more than 100 trucks; ASI: 18 kits).

### Cited Findings
- MarketsandMarkets treats retrofit kits and brownfield versus greenfield as buyer considerations, but does not size them. — [MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/autonomous-mining-haulage-systems-market-266401441.html)
- A single large haul truck can cost up to about $5M. Retrofit targets sites where replacing trucks would be too expensive. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/b80dbfbe-1f47-4ec2-b86c-39500a13efcf/) (secondary source)
- Komatsu reports more than 15% lower load-and-haul unit costs with AHS (vendor claim). — [OEM Off-Highway / Komatsu](https://www.oemoffhighway.com/electronics/smart-systems/automated-systems/press-release/20990506/komatsu-america-corp-komatsu-celebrates-10th-anniversary-of-commercial-deployment-of-autonomous-haulage-system)
- An industry analysis expects retrofit adoption to start where replacement budgets are tight and fleets are mixed (a forecast). — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/b80dbfbe-1f47-4ec2-b86c-39500a13efcf/)

### Inferences
- The retrofit and aftermarket market has to be built bottom-up: number of eligible trucks and excavators × kit price, plus software subscription per machine per year. Top-down analyst figures won't support it.
- Skillings.net publishes detailed ROI and cost-per-tonne figures for 2026 but cites no primary data. I left them out.

### Gaps
- I found no public per-truck price for a retrofit kit, whether from Pronto, ASI, SafeAI or EACON.
- I found no analyst estimate for the market for excavator autonomy or excavator retrofit.
