Built today and running: a pipeline from a drone scan to a simulator with roads, berms and grades; NVIDIA Isaac Sim with cameras, lidar, rigged excavators and a keep-in-bounds safety layer; voice radio dispatch where machines talk back; the live ops view; and an RL training environment. Ready for hardware: the sim sensors match the pod's camera and lidar spec, the sim and a real truck use the same protocol, and the policy outputs throttle and steer for a drive-by-wire kit. Next: the pod hardware and the ISO 17757 safety case.
