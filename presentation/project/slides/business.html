Sources: GAIN 2024; Boliden/Komatsu 2021; MSHA 2023–26; BLS 2025; USGS/UEPG. Market size and payback are our model.

Quarries move 39 billion tonnes a year with a driver in every truck. OEM autonomy costs about 2.3 million per truck and is built for giant mines. Powered haulage is 25 to 43 percent of US mining deaths. On a quarry truck the driver is 25 to 35 percent of the running cost. Operators with autonomy report 13 to 20 percent lower haul cost and BHP saw 90 percent fewer serious truck incidents. The serviceable market is 0.3 to 1.2 billion a year. We sell a 90k kit, onboarding and 4k per truck per month; a 10-truck quarry on two shifts pays back in about a year, four months at 24/7.
