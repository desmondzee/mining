On a quarry-sized truck the driver is 25 to 35 percent of hourly running cost, roughly double the share on big mine trucks. Rio Tinto and BHP reported 13 to 20 percent lower haul cost with autonomy, and BHP saw over 90 percent fewer significant truck incidents. With our pricing a 10-truck quarry pays back in about a year on two shifts, four months at 24/7.
