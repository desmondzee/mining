How it works

A drone flight to an autonomous site

Scan

Drone photogrammetry of any quarry

Simulate

Roads, berms, grades to industry standard in seconds; NVIDIA Isaac Sim

Train

Vision policy learns on camera + lidar in the sim

Talk

ElevenLabs + LLM turn speech into goals; machines reply

Fly a drone over the site; in seconds it becomes a simulator with haul roads, berms and grades sized to the real truck. The vision policy trains there on camera and lidar. On site, ElevenLabs transcribes the radio, an LLM turns it into goals, and each machine answers back.
