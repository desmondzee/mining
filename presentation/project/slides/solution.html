The solution

Retrofit the trucks they own. Run the site by voice.

  • A sensor pod bolts onto existing trucks and excavators: cameras, lidar, compute
  • A vision policy drives; a safety layer keeps trucks on the road, and “Everyone stop” halts the site
  • Dispatch over radio in plain speech; every machine answers back
  • New site = a drone flight; fleet footage keeps the site sim current
R² retrofits the trucks and excavators a quarry already owns with a sensor pod, and you run the site by talking to it. A vision policy drives; you dispatch in plain speech and each machine answers in its own voice. The image is a concept render of the pod.
