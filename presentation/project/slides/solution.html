The solution

Retrofit the trucks they own. Run the site by voice.

  • Bolt-on sensor pod
  • Vision policy drives
  • Radio dispatch by voice
  • Safety stop built in
R² bolts a sensor pod, with cameras, lidar and compute, onto the trucks and excavators a quarry already owns. A vision policy drives, and a safety layer keeps trucks on the road. You dispatch over the radio in plain speech, every machine answers back, and “Everyone stop” halts the whole site. A new site is a drone flight, and fleet footage keeps the site simulation current.
