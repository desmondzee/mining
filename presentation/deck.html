The solution

Retrofit the trucks they own. Run the site by voice.

  • A sensor pod bolts onto existing trucks and excavators: cameras, lidar, compute
  • A vision policy drives on camera and lidar
  • Dispatch over radio in plain speech; every machine answers back

Customer economics

On a 55–65 t quarry truck the operator is 25–35% of hourly running cost, versus 12–15% on big mine trucks.

Autonomy operators report 13–20% lower load-and-haul cost and 700–1,000 extra hours per truck a year.
