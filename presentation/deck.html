The solution

Retrofit the trucks they own. Run the site by voice.

  • A sensor pod bolts onto existing trucks and excavators: cameras, lidar, compute
  • A vision policy drives; a safety layer keeps trucks on the road, and “Everyone stop” halts the site
  • Dispatch over radio in plain speech; every machine answers back
  • New site = a drone flight; fleet footage keeps the site sim current

Customer economics

The driver is the cost autonomy removes

On a 55–65 t quarry truck the operator is 25–35% of hourly running cost, versus 12–15% on big mine trucks.

Autonomy operators report 13–20% lower load-and-haul cost and 700–1,000 extra hours per truck a year.

Safety: BHP saw 90%+ fewer significant truck incidents with autonomy; nobody in the cab at the face.

  • NVIDIA Isaac Sim + Unity: RTX cameras, lidar, rigged excavators, tipping beds, keep-in-bounds

Ready for the physical layer
