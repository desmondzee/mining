## 3. World Labs Marble

Sources: https://docs.worldlabs.ai/marble/models.md, https://docs.worldlabs.ai/api/models.md, https://docs.worldlabs.ai/marble/create/prompt-guides/image-prompt.md, https://docs.worldlabs.ai/api/world-generation-examples.md, https://help.scenario.com/articles/4774055514-marble-by-world-labs-the-essentials

**Key facts**
- **Models (API `model`):** `marble-1.1-plus`, `marble-1.1`, `marble-1.0` and `marble-1.0-draft`. **If you omit the field, the API still defaults to `marble-1.0`, so always set it.**
  - **Use `marble-1.1-plus` for the pit.** The docs say it "automatically expands its coverage of 3D space to generate bigger worlds". It costs 1,500 credits plus a variable 0–1,500 and is the slowest.
  - `marble-1.1` is aimed at "contained interiors and single locations" and costs a fixed 1,500 credits.
  - Use `marble-1.0-draft` (150 credits) to iterate on prompts and seeds first, then rerun the same seed on 1.1-plus.
- **Endpoint:** `POST https://api.worldlabs.ai/marble/v1/worlds:generate` with header `WLT-Api-Key`. The body is `{display_name, model, world_prompt:{type:"text"|"image"|"multi-image", text_prompt, image_prompt:{source:"uri"|"media_asset", uri|media_asset_id}, is_pano}}`.
- **Input specs:** text up to 2,000 characters. Images should be about 1024 px on the long edge, 16:9 to 9:16, png preferred, at most 20 MB. A panorama must be 2:1 equirectangular, about 2560 px wide.
- **Text vs image vs pano:** a text prompt is converted to a panorama first, then to a world. **A single image gives the most control** over the look, so generate a concept with gpt-image and feed it in. A true 360° pano gives the widest coverage but must have a seamless edge and full sky and ground coverage. Multi-image input (2–8 views of the same place) gives the best reconstruction if you can produce consistent views.
- **Viewpoint:** the docs never mention aerial images. They ask for "visible ground planes", "clear depth and perspective" and a balance of foreground, midground and background. That points to **an elevated ground-level oblique view, roughly eye height to about 20 m, looking across the pit.** A top-down or orthographic image gives the model almost no depth cues and tends to produce a flat or warped world. Generate from the oblique view and use your own aerial camera in three.js.
- **Known limitations**
  - The world is centred on the input viewpoint. Quality drops with distance from it, and the far side of a 400 m pit will be soft or blobby. Use Expand, Compose or multiple worlds to fill the gaps.
  - There is no documented maximum size in metres. Since January 2026, outputs are "roughly scaled ... to real-world units", but still calibrate `splat_transform.scale` from the collider.
  - People and animals render poorly, so leave them out of prompts and images. Text and signage come out garbled.
  - Camera directions in a text prompt do nothing. Describe the place, not the camera.
  - Very short prompts get recaptioned. Write full descriptions, or set the recaption option off if the API exposes it.
  - Describing absence ("no people") tends to add the thing you excluded.

## 4. Reactor LingBot World 2 (driver's-eye stream)

Sources: https://docs.reactor.inc/model-api-reference/lingbot-world-2/schema, /overview, /prompt-guide.md
Model `reactor/lingbot-world-2` outputs 1664×960 at 48 fps. Commands apply at the next chunk boundary and **persist until set back to `"idle"`.**

**Commands** (`reactor.sendCommand(name, params)` or the typed `LingbotWorld2Model` methods)
| Purpose | Command | Param: values |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt (hot-swappable) | `set_prompt` | `prompt`: string |
| Anchor image (required before start) | `set_image` | `image`: FileRef from `await reactor.uploadFile(file)`. Wait for the `image_accepted` message. |
| W / S | `set_move_longitudinal` | `move_longitudinal`: `"idle"` / `"forward"` / `"back"` |
| A / D (strafe) | `set_move_lateral` | `move_lateral`: `"idle"` / `"strafe_left"` / `"strafe_right"` |
| Look / steer yaw | `set_look_horizontal` | `look_horizontal`: `"idle"` / `"left"` / `"right"` |
| Look pitch | `set_look_vertical` | `look_vertical`: `"idle"` / `"up"` / `"down"` |
| Turn rate | `set_rotation_speed_deg` | `rotation_speed_deg`: float 0–30 (default 5, degrees per latent frame) |
| Direct pose | `set_camera_pose` | `camera_pose`: flat array of per-frame `[rx,ry,rz,tx,ty,tz]` deltas, length a multiple of 6, at most 1536. The rotation overrides look and the translation adds to WASD. Pass `[]` to turn it off. |
| Lifecycle | `start`, `pause`, `resume`, `reset`; also `set_seed` (`seed` int, default 42) | `reset` clears the image and prompt. |
| Advanced | `set_attn_window` (`"auto"`/`"small"`/`"large"`), `set_kv_cache_reset` (`mode`: `"off"`/`"auto"`/`"manual"`), `trigger_kv_cache_reset` | |
| Messages | `{type, data}`: `image_accepted`, `prompt_accepted`, `conditions_ready`, `generation_started`, `chunk_complete`, `command_error`, `state` | |

**Mapping the contract `drive` to commands:** `longitudinal: forward` maps to `set_move_longitudinal forward`. Map **`lateral: left/right` to `set_look_horizontal left/right`** (a truck steers by yawing, not strafing). Set `rotation_speed_deg` to about `clamp(|yaw_rate_dps| / 48 * k, 2, 10)`. Send `idle` when the drive goes idle. Only send commands when the value changes. Keep presses short, because long holds drift. Inputs sent in the first seconds of a session may be dropped.

**Prompt guide rules:** build each prompt as `base + camera + movement`. The base is at most about 600 characters and has no motion verbs and no point-of-view words. Pin 2–4 landmarks with counts and positions. For first person, name a foreground anchor (the dashboard or hood) that stays centred. Don't describe absence ("empty road"). Keep the whole prompt under about 2000 characters.

**Driver's-eye prompts**
1. *Hauling, dry, first person:*
   `The yellow hood and black dashboard of a giant mining haul truck fill the bottom of the frame, two side mirrors at the edges. Ahead, a wide graded gravel haul road curves down the terraced benches of an open-pit copper mine, layered ochre and rust rock walls on the right, a rubble safety berm on the left, one excavator loading ore at the bench far ahead. Bright midday sun, dust haze, photorealistic. The hood stays centred at the bottom of the frame as the view advances along the road; the tyres roll over loose gravel and dust trails behind.`
2. *Climbing the ramp with a load toward the crusher:*
   `From the cab of a loaded mining dump truck, the yellow hood fills the lower frame. A steep switchback gravel ramp climbs the pit wall toward a corrugated steel crusher building with an inclined conveyor on the rim, two white fuel tanks beside it, striated tan rock benches on both sides. Late-afternoon golden light, long shadows, fine dust, realistic. The hood stays centred while the road advances steadily uphill; gravel crunches under the tyres.`
3. *Flooded pit (hot-swap with `set_prompt` during the flood beat):*
   `From the cab of a mining haul truck, the mud-streaked yellow hood fills the lower frame. A wet gravel haul road with wide brown puddles descends toward the flooded pit floor, murky water covering the lowest bench, wet dark grey rock walls glistening, one orange water pump with hoses at the water's edge. Overcast sky, drizzle, low mist, photorealistic. The hood stays centred as the view advances slowly; tyres splash through shallow puddles.`

**Anchor image:**
- 16:9 landscape, ideally rendered at 1664×960 or upscaled to that.
- Same nouns and landmarks as the base prompt. The image wins over the text, and a mismatch shows up as artifacts.
- First-person cab view with the hood or dashboard as a consistent foreground anchor, road vanishing point near the centre, horizon in the upper third.
- Sharp, well lit, no motion blur, no text or UI overlays.
- Generate one per scenario (dry, ramp, flooded) with gpt-image using the same wording as the prompt. Optionally, render a frame from the three.js truck camera and run it through gpt-image to match the Marble world.
